Unified library

Browse your games
in one place.

Connect your Steam, GOG, Epic Games, and Xbox accounts. Search and sort your collection in a clean, easy-to-browse interface, or filter it by store or list.

  • ▤

    Custom lists

    Group games into your own lists and swipe between them. Keep favorites and games you want to play next easy to find.

  • ✓

    Track your progress

    Mark games as Unplayed, Playing, or Finished to keep track of what you have started and completed.

  • ♡

    Your own wishlist

    Add games to a custom wishlist across stores. You can also import your Steam wishlist.

HowLongToBeat & Metascores

Compare playtime and scores.

Check HowLongToBeat completion-time estimates and Metascores to compare games and find one that fits your available time. Open a game to browse its screenshots and watch YouTube videos.

Choose artwork with SteamGridDB

Pick the cover you want for each game using SteamGridDB and your own API key. Selected artwork is saved for offline browsing.

Search the Steam store

Search the Steam catalog and use community tags to narrow the results. You can also browse Xbox Game Pass titles.

Browse offline

Once loaded, your library and artwork remain available without internet. Your lists, wishlist, and progress are saved on your device.

Game file export

Export games to Downloads.

Download games you own on Steam, GOG, or Epic Games as a ZIP file or game folder in your Android Downloads folder. Use the game data with compatible Android engine ports for games such as Morrowind or Diablo 1.

Set up the files in your chosen engine port to play. Xbox supports library browsing only.

Local storage

Saved on your device.

Your collection and preferences are stored locally. GameFibre doesn’t save your passwords or sign-in codes. Saved account sessions and your SteamGridDB key are encrypted using Android Keystore.

Connecting accounts, refreshing your collection, downloading games, and loading new media need an internet connection.
