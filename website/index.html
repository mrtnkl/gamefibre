Why GameFibre?

GameFibre takes a simpler approach to game libraries. Less about statistics and managing backlogs, more about enjoying your collection.

  • Offline first

    Your collection, covers and game details remain available offline, even when store connections expire.

  • Custom artwork

    Choose alternative game covers from SteamGridDB to personalize your library.

  • Favorites, lists and progress

    Mark favorites, create custom lists and track which games you're playing or have completed.

  • Sorting and discovery

    Sort by Metascore, playtime or HowLongToBeat, or randomize your collection to find something unexpected.

  • Download original game files

    Download games you own directly to Android as ZIP files, for use with ports and engines like OpenMW (Morrowind) and DevilutionX (Diablo).
